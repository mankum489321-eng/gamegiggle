GameGiggle Family

gamegiggle-uploader is the private automation tool behind the GameGiggle Family YouTube channel. It publishes the channel owner's own licensed short-form videos to that one channel on a daily schedule.

It is not a public product. There is no sign-up, no user accounts and no shared interface — it authenticates as the single Google Account that owns the channel and acts only on that account.
